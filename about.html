About us

People helping people.

Generation Grace exists to mobilise compassion into practical support for communities.

Our purpose

Generation Grace focuses on community support, health and disaster relief. We want to recruit volunteers, share impact stories, provide clear information about our programmes and reach as many people as we can.

Our approach

Understand community needs before designing support.

03

Turn resources and skills into practical action.

04

Share

Communicate programmes and impact responsibly.
